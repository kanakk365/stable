# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

Infrastructure investors, developers, and charge point operators (CPOs) who are deciding where to build EV charging and how to run the networks they already have. They read this page at a desk during the working day, often while preparing an investment memo or a network review, and they are comparing Stable against spreadsheets, consultants, and other forecasting tools.

## Product Purpose

Stable Auto forecasts and optimizes EV charging performance. **Evaluate** identifies high-potential sites and forecasts utilization and ROI before anything is built. **Operate** monitors utilization, pricing, and reliability across live networks so operators can benchmark and improve them. Success for this page is a visitor booking a demo ("See it in action") or contacting sales.

## Positioning

Investment-grade EV charging forecasts built on real charging operating data, used before you build and after you deploy.

## Conversion & proof

- Primary and secondary CTA: "See it in action" (product tour / demo); "Contact sales". Later on the page, "Schedule a call" for a 30-minute introductory conversation.
- The line a visitor remembers after 10 seconds: Smarter forecasting and performance for EV charging.
- Belief ladder: most charging stations underperform (80% of charging happens at 30% of stations) → that gap is predictable with the right data → Stable's forecasts come from real operating data and are trusted by serious investors and CPOs → it covers both the build decision and live operations → a 30-minute call is low-risk.
- Proof on hand: customer logo wall (`public/logos/`), MN8 testimonial (Senior Director, EV Charging), scale figures (23,000+ charging locations evaluated, 50,000+ active chargers under analysis, $5B+ infrastructure investment planned), and the Pricing, Utilization, and Gas Price Equivalent indices (Oct '25).

## Brand Personality

Precise, calm, investment-grade. The voice of a careful analyst who shows the data rather than shouting about it. Visitors should feel confidence and clarity: this is a team that has done the measuring.

## Anti-references

The dark, near-black treatment shared by the sibling reference sites in `../` (this site must read lighter, in daylight). A pure white page. Generic SaaS gradient heroes and stock "EV plugged into a car" imagery.

## Design Principles

- Show the data. Every claim on the page is backed by a visual that behaves like the product: charts, maps, forecasts.
- Daylight, not glare. A light ground that is never stark white; contrast comes from deep teal bands at a few chosen moments.
- One system, before and after the build. Evaluate and Operate are a lifecycle, and the page presents them in that order.
- Investment-grade restraint. Motion and color earn their place; nothing decorative competes with a number.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA: body text at 4.5:1 or better, visible focus states, keyboard-reachable navigation and menus. Every animation has a reduced-motion alternative, and chart meaning never relies on color alone.
